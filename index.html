Questions

How do I order custom decorated cookies, a cake or cake pops?

Fill out our custom order form with your date, theme, colors and any inspiration photos. We’ll reply with a quote, then send a secure invoice you can pay online. Start a custom order.

How far ahead should I order?

At least 2 weeks ahead. Orders 7 to 13 days out can be placed as a rush order for an extra 20%.

Where do I pick up my order?

Pickup is at Walmart Neighborhood Market (Rock Creek Rd & 36th Ave NW, Norman) or Casey’s (SW 149th St & S Western Ave, south OKC). Pickup times are Thursday and Friday 6–8:30 PM and Saturday 9 AM–noon. You pick a time when you order.

Do you deliver?

Just ask. Add a note to your order or custom request, and a delivery fee may be added at Sugar Lane’s discretion.

How much are decorated sugar cookies?

Custom decorated cookie sets start at $45 a dozen for standard designs and $60 a dozen for detailed designs with hand-painting, many colors or custom shapes. Each set is quoted after you send your idea.

How much are your other treats?

Classic cookies are $22 a dozen and specialty cookies are $25 a dozen. Cake pops are $18 a dozen (2 dozen minimum per flavor), cupcakes start at $22 a dozen, and custom cakes start at $30. Party packs are $75 for 3 dozen or $120 for 5 dozen.

When is my card charged?

When you order online, we place a hold on your card and review your order within 24 hours. Your card is only charged once we confirm it; if we can’t make it, the hold is released.

How can I pay?

Online orders take credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Cash App Pay.

What is your cancellation policy?

Let us know at least 7 days before pickup for a full refund. Cancellations within 7 days of pickup are not refunded.

Do your treats contain allergens?

Our kitchen uses wheat, eggs, dairy, peanuts and tree nuts, so we can’t guarantee any item is allergen-free.

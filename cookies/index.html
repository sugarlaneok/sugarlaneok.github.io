Custom cookie questions

Where can I get custom decorated cookies in Norman, OK?

Sugar Lane is a home bakery in Norman, Oklahoma run by Laney and her mom, Kelly. We make royal-iced sugar cookies designed around your theme, colors and names. Send your idea on our custom order form and we’ll reply with a quote. Pickup is in Norman (Walmart Neighborhood Market, Rock Creek Rd & 36th Ave NW) or south OKC (Casey’s, SW 149th St & S Western Ave), which is an easy drive from Moore and Oklahoma City.

How much do custom decorated cookies cost?

Custom cookie sets start at $45 a dozen for standard designs and $60 a dozen for detailed designs with hand-painting, many colors or custom shapes. Every set is quoted after you send your idea.

What occasions do you make custom cookies for?

Baby showers, gender reveals, birthdays, first birthdays, graduations, bridal showers, bachelorette parties, weddings, engagements, teacher appreciation, holidays and business events. If you can describe it, we can design cookies for it.

Do you make Halloween, Christmas and other holiday cookies?

Yes. We run limited seasonal presales for holidays like Halloween, Christmas and Valentine’s Day, with decorated cookie boxes you can order online. See what’s open on our seasonal page, or request a custom holiday set any time.

Can I just order cookies by the dozen?

Yes. Classic cookies are $22 a dozen and specialty cookies are $25 a dozen, and you can order them online for pickup.
